Suraasa B2B Partnership Collaterals

A unified presentation system spanning three B2B narratives: the SURE school subscription program, the Hire recruitment service, and a co-branded Rotary District 3234 partnership proposal. The constraint was that these decks are presented live by sales teams but also forwarded as leave-behinds, so every layout is built to one idea per slide, legible without a presenter, with a shared grammar of persistent footers, gradient section dividers, and modular card grids flexing across all three stories. Content was provided; the visual narrative, layout system, and end-to-end design were crafted from the ground up. The decks became the standard collateral for Suraasa's school partnerships pipeline and supported successful partner conversations, including the Rotary District engagement they were built to win.
