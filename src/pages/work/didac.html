Suraasa at Didac

A comprehensive visual identity and collateral suite for Suraasa's “For the Love of Teaching” campaign at the Didac exhibition, spanning data-driven program brochures to tactile keepsakes like gradient bookmarks and pins. The core tension was speaking to three very different audiences (governments, schools, and teachers) from one booth, so the system uses a vibrant yet structured editorial aesthetic with clean typography that lets each audience self-select the depth they need without cognitive overload. The collateral became a high-traffic draw at the booth and continues to be reused across subsequent Suraasa events, outliving the exhibition it was built for.
