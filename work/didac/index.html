Didac Branding

A visual identity and collateral suite for a teaching campaign at the Didac exhibition, from data-driven program brochures to keepsakes like gradient bookmarks and pins. The core tension was speaking to three very different audiences (governments, schools, and teachers) from one booth, so the system uses a vibrant yet structured editorial look with clean typography that lets each audience self-select the depth they need. The collateral became a high-traffic draw at the booth and keeps being reused at later events, outliving the exhibition it was built for.
