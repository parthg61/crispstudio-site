Recruitment Platform

The problem

Hiring ran across too many tools. Recruiters switched between three or four of them for every candidate and lost 30–45 minutes per candidate moving data by hand. Partner schools received profiles by email, so feedback was slow. Teachers heard nothing after applying, and some waited more than 60 days for a status update.

How we solved it

We designed one synchronised hiring system with three connected views: a recruiter workspace built around the hiring pipeline, a read-only portal for schools, and a progress dashboard for teachers. When a recruiter moves a candidate, every view updates at once. Recruiters keep ownership of every hiring decision, schools see the same pipeline and comment in context, and teachers see every stage of their application.

One action. One source of truth. Three connected experiences.
